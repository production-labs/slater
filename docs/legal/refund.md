Slater subscriptions are sold by our reseller, **Paddle.com**, the Merchant of Record
for all our orders. Paddle processes refunds. This policy works alongside Paddle's
buyer terms; where the law gives you more rights, those apply.

## 14-day refund on new subscriptions

If Slater isn't right for you, ask for a refund within **14 days** of your **first
purchase** of a monthly or yearly plan and we'll refund it in full. No questions
asked.

## Renewals

- **Yearly plans:** we email you a reminder before each yearly renewal. If you forget
  to cancel and your yearly plan renews, ask within **14 days** of the renewal and
  we'll refund that renewal in full.
- **Monthly plans:** renewals aren't refunded. Cancel any time to stop the next one;
  you keep access until the end of the month you've paid for.

## Other situations

- If we close your account without cause, or stop offering Slater, we refund the
  unused part of any prepaid subscription.
- If something went wrong with a charge (for example you were charged twice), contact
  us and we'll fix it.

## How to ask for a refund

Email **hello@productionlabs.io** with the email address on your account, or reply to
your Paddle receipt. You can also contact Paddle directly at paddle.net. Refunds go
back to your original payment method; how long they take to appear depends on your
bank.

A refund cancels your subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you
had paid for, and your account then follows the cancellation steps in our
[Terms of Service](/terms) (section 5): a 30-day grace period, then deletion.
