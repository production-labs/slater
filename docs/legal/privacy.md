# Slater Privacy Policy

**DRAFT, not reviewed by a lawyer. Not in effect.**

Last updated: [CONFIRM: date when published]

This policy explains what information **Production Labs LLC** ("we", "us") collects
through Slater (slater.productionlabs.io, the "Service"), what we do with it, who we
share it with, and the choices you have.

**The short version:**
- We collect what we need to run Slater, and nothing for advertising.
- We don't sell your information, and we don't send marketing emails.
- We use a small number of service providers (listed below) to host, store, email,
  scan receipts, and take payments.
- Files sent through client portals are deleted after 14 days.
- You can see, export, correct, or delete your information.

---

## 1. Who this covers, and our role

- **Slater customers** ("you"): people with a Slater account.
- **People in a customer's Slater account**: crew, talent, clients, Key Personnel,
  and others whose details a customer adds.
- **Portal Visitors**: people a customer shares a client portal with.
- **Visitors** to our website and login pages.

For your account information, **we decide how it's used** (in privacy-law terms, we're
the "controller"). For information customers put into Slater about other people,
**the customer decides**, and we process it on their behalf (we're a "processor"). If
you're in a customer's Slater account or a Portal Visitor and have questions about how
your information is used for a production, please ask that customer; we'll help them
answer. [LAWYER: Data Processing Agreement to accompany this.]

## 2. What we collect

**Account information** (from you):
- name, email address, company or business name, phone number, time zone, and logo;
- your password, stored only as a secure one-way hash (we can't see it);
- your license key and plan.

**Device information** (to apply your plan's device limit): for each device you sign
in on, a device identifier generated by Slater, the browser and operating system it
reports, and when it was last used.

**Billing information**: payments are handled by our reseller, Paddle (section 4). We
receive your name, email, country, plan, and payment status. **We never see or store
your card details.**

**Content you add**: projects, schedules, crew, talent and Key Personnel details,
contacts, organizations, locations, workback items, notes, expenses, and receipt
images. This often includes other people's names, phone numbers, email addresses, and
roles.

**Client portal information**: when a Portal Visitor uses a portal, we store what they
send: the name they type, approvals and change requests with any notes, and files they
upload (with file names and sizes).

**Technical information**:
- a login cookie (section 6);
- IP addresses, which our hosting provider may record in its request logs, and which
  we use for a short time to limit repeated requests (for example, password guesses).
  Our own app logs record which page or action was requested, not who you are, and
  never record client portal or download links.

**Messages**: if you email us, we keep the conversation so we can help you.

We don't use analytics tools, advertising networks, tracking pixels, or social media
plug-ins, and we don't buy information about you from anyone.

## 3. How we use it

- **To provide Slater**: run your account, save and show your projects, create
  documents you ask for, run client portals you set up, and send the emails Slater
  needs to send (section 5).
- **To scan receipts** you choose to scan, using our AI provider (section 4).
- **To keep Slater secure**: apply device limits, block abuse, and investigate
  problems.
- **To bill you**, through Paddle.
- **To support you** when you contact us.
- **To meet legal obligations**, and to protect our rights if there's a dispute.

We **don't sell** your information, **don't share it for advertising**, and **don't
use your content to train AI models**.

**Legal bases** (for people in the EU, UK, and similar places): performing our
contract with you (running your account and the Service); our legitimate interests in
keeping Slater secure and working well, and in processing information about others on
our customers' instructions; legal obligations; and consent where we ask for it.

We share information only with these service providers, only as needed to run Slater,
and under agreements that require them to protect it:

| Provider | What they do for us | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Railway** | Hosts the Slater application and its database | United States |

We may also share information:
- **with people you choose**: for example, portal content with Portal Visitors you
  share a link with, and portal notices with Key Personnel you add to them;
- **if the law requires it**, or to protect people's safety or our rights; we'll push
  back on requests that aren't legally valid;
- **if the business is sold or merged**, in which case this policy continues to apply
  to your information, and we'll tell you.

## 5. Emails

Slater only sends emails connected to using the Service:
- password resets;
- client portal notices: when a client sends files, approves, or asks for changes, to
  the account owner and any Key Personnel the owner chooses for that project. These
  include download links that work without signing in until the file is deleted, so
  they shouldn't be forwarded;
- important notices about your account, billing, or changes to our terms.

We **don't send marketing emails** and **don't track** whether emails are opened or
links clicked. [CONFIRM: open/click tracking is off in Resend.] If you were added to a
project's portal notices and want to stop them, ask the producer who added you, or
email us.

## 6. Cookies and similar technology

Slater uses only what it needs to work, so no cookie banner is needed:

| Name / item | What it's for | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Login cookie (`connect.sid`) | Keeps you signed in | 24 hours |
| Portal password cookie (`slater_portal`) | Remembers that a Portal Visitor entered a portal's password | 30 days |
| Browser storage: your settings | Remembers the last project you opened, your sort order and views, and your workback templates and defaults | Until you clear it |
| Browser storage: unsaved work | A temporary copy of changes and your recent projects' call sheets, so nothing is lost if your connection drops; cleared once saved | Until saved, or you clear it |
| Browser storage: open tabs | Stops two open Slater tabs from overwriting each other's changes | While Slater is open |
| Browser storage: portal name and timeline view | Remembers the name a Portal Visitor typed, so they don't retype it, and the timeline zoom they picked | Until they clear it |

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. Our fonts are served from our own
server.
Payment pages run by Paddle may set their own cookies; see Paddle's policy.

## 7. How long we keep it

| Information | Kept |
|---|---|
| Account information and content | While your account is active or paused |
| Paused accounts | Up to 12 months; we email you at least 30 days before deleting |
| Cancelled accounts | 30 days after the paid period ends, then deleted |
| Deleted accounts | Removed from live systems within 30 days |
| Database backups | 90 days, then overwritten (deleted data disappears from backups within 90 days) |
| Client portal files | 14 days after upload, or sooner if the producer deletes them |
| Client portal approvals and notes | As part of the producer's project |
| IP addresses for limiting requests | About 15 minutes, in memory |
| Hosting request logs | As long as our hosting provider keeps them [CONFIRM: Railway log retention for our plan] |
| Support emails | Up to 24 months after the conversation ends |
| Billing records | Kept by Paddle as required by tax law |

We may keep information longer if the law requires it or to resolve a dispute.

## 8. How we protect it

All connections to Slater are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored as one-way
hashes. Client portal and download links are long random or signed codes, portals can
have passwords, and links stop working when files are deleted. Access to production
systems is limited to the people who run Slater. Backups are stored separately from
the live database. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your
information, we'll tell you as the law requires.

## 9. Where your information is processed

Slater is run from the United States, and our providers process information mainly
in the United States. If you're outside the US, your information will be transferred
there. Where the law requires, we rely on approved safeguards such as the European
Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. [LAWYER: confirm transfer mechanism.]

## 10. Your rights and choices

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:
- **see** the information we hold about you, and get a **copy** in a portable format;
- **correct** it;
- **delete** it (you can also delete your account yourself; section 7 explains timing);
- **restrict or object** to some uses of it;
- **withdraw consent**, where we relied on it.

Email hello@productionlabs.io. We'll confirm who you are before acting, and reply
within 30 days (we may extend that where the law allows, and will tell you). If your
information is in a customer's Slater account, we may pass your request to that
customer, who decides what happens to it.

**US state privacy laws** (for example California's): we don't sell or "share"
personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we don't use
sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you. We won't treat you
differently for using your privacy rights.

**EU and UK:** you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

## 11. Children

Slater is for people **18 and older**. We don't knowingly collect information from
children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). If you believe a child has given us
information, contact us and we'll delete it.

## 12. Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes, we'll email account holders and post a notice in
Slater before they take effect, and update the date at the top.
