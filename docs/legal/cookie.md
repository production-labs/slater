# Slater Cookie Policy

**DRAFT, not reviewed by a lawyer. Not in effect.**

Last updated: [CONFIRM: date when published]

This policy explains the cookies and similar browser storage that **Production Labs
LLC** ("we", "us") uses on Slater (slater.productionlabs.io, the "Service"), and the
choices you have. It should be read alongside our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

**The short version:**
- We only use cookies and browser storage Slater needs to work.
- We don't use advertising or analytics cookies, and we don't show a cookie banner,
  because we don't use anything that requires your consent.
- You can clear cookies and browser storage at any time in your browser; some things
  will stop working until you sign in or enter a portal password again.

---

## 1. What this covers

"Cookies" are small files a website asks your browser to store. Browsers also offer
similar storage (sometimes called local storage or session storage) that works the
same way for our purposes. This policy covers both, on Slater itself and on client
portal pages. Payment pages run by Paddle, our payment reseller, are not covered here;
see Paddle's own cookie and privacy policies.

## 2. Why we don't need a cookie banner

Most places that require cookie banners or consent tools make an exception for cookies
that are "strictly necessary" to provide the service you asked for, such as staying
signed in. Every cookie and storage item we use falls into that category: nothing
below is used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or tracking you across other
sites. That's why Slater doesn't show a cookie consent banner.

## 3. Cookies we use

| Name | What it's for | Who it applies to | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| `connect.sid` | Keeps you signed in to your Slater account | Slater account holders | 24 hours, or until you sign out |
| `slater_portal` | Remembers that a Portal Visitor correctly entered a client portal's password, so they aren't asked again on every page | Client portal visitors | 30 days |

Both cookies are essential: without them you'd be asked to sign in, or re-enter a
portal password, on every page.

## 4. Browser storage we use

In addition to cookies, Slater stores some information directly in your browser
(not sent to any other website):

| Item | What it's for | Who it applies to | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| Your settings | Remembers the last project you opened, your sort order and views, and your workback templates and defaults | Account holders | Until you clear it |
| Unsaved work | A temporary copy of changes and your recent projects' call sheets, so nothing is lost if your connection drops; cleared once saved | Account holders | Until saved, or you clear it |
| Open tabs marker | Stops two open Slater tabs from overwriting each other's changes | Account holders | While Slater is open |
| Portal name and timeline view | Remembers the name a Portal Visitor typed, so they don't retype it, and the timeline zoom they picked | Client portal visitors | Until they clear it |

## 5. What we don't use

We don't use advertising cookies, analytics cookies (such as Google Analytics),
tracking pixels, social media plug-ins, or any cookie or script that follows you to
other websites. We don't buy or sell cookie data.

Slater's fonts are served from our own server, not a third-party font service.

## 6. Your choices

You can control or delete cookies and browser storage through your browser's
settings. If you do:
- clearing `connect.sid`, or your browser's site data for Slater, will sign you out;
- clearing `slater_portal` means a client portal will ask for its password again;
- clearing browser storage will forget your saved settings and any unsaved work not
  yet synced to our servers, and will clear a portal visitor's typed name.

Blocking all cookies for Slater will prevent the Service from working, since signing
in depends on `connect.sid`.

## 7. Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes to the cookies or browser storage we use, we'll update
this page and the date at the top.
